# AutoTip
<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/143406151a5ff4959e153a497855fee37cb1a2ae.png" alt="AutoTip icon" width="64">

AutoTip is a Hypixel mod that automatically earns you free XP & coins passively.

Every 30 minutes, the mod automatically tips all active network boosters and other online AutoTip users. By sending tips, you join an active pool of players tipping each other, which greatly increases your passive profits compared to simple macros.

**How does it work?**

Unlike other AutoTip mods that simply automate the `/tip all` command, which does nothing if no Network Boosters are active, this mod goes much further by using a custom API. By sending tips through this API, your client enters a rotation where thousands of other active users tip you back. This ensures you get tipped back, giving you a constant flow of XP & coins that basic macros completely miss.

**Commands**

``/autotip or /at`` - Shows general info.

``/autotip info`` - Shows connection info.

``/autotip stats [period]`` - Shows tipping stats [in a specific period].

``/autotip currency [period]`` - Shows the amount of currency earned [in a specific period].

``/autotip messages [mode]`` - Set message visibility.
